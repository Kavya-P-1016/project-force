Managing leachate shock loads without destabilizing biology

Equalization, staged dosing, and control points that protect biological performance when incoming leachate strength spikes unexpectedly.

Shock loads are predictable in pattern

Seasonal rainfall, cell turnover, and haul schedule changes create spikes. Design and operate for those spikes instead of treating them as surprises.

Equalization strategy

Volume buffering alone is not enough. Track COD, ammonia, and pH trends in the equalization tank and adjust return rates before biology sees the hit.

Biology protection

Use staged nutrient dosing, dissolved oxygen alarms, and temporary bypass plans that keep biomass alive during recovery.

Practical checklist

  • Size equalization for peak week, not average week.
  • Monitor ammonia and COD trends before biological reactors.
  • Pre agree bypass and recirculation protocols with operators.
  • Keep a shock load log tied to weather and landfill activity.
