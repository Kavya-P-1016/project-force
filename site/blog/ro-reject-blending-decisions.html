RO reject blending decisions that reduce downstream failures

Field tested blending ranges and monitoring thresholds to avoid scaling, upset events, and avoidable shutdowns in reuse lines.

Blending is a design decision

RO reject is not waste by default. When blended back into lower salinity streams, it can reduce freshwater demand. The wrong blend ratio, however, creates scaling events that are expensive to reverse.

Field tested ranges

Start with conservative blend ratios and online conductivity guardrails. Increase only after stable operation across seasonal temperature swings.

Instrumentation that pays off

Install blend ratio logging, downstream hardness checks, and automatic diversion when thresholds are crossed.

Practical checklist

  • Define maximum blend ratio by receiving stream chemistry.
  • Use online conductivity and LSI guardrails with auto diversion.
  • Review blend performance weekly during the first operating quarter.
  • Document every scaling event with blend history for root cause review.
