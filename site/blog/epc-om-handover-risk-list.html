EPC to O&M handover risk list for smoother plant startup

A structured handover checklist that closes documentation gaps, stabilizes commissioning, and reduces early life operational risk.

Handover failures are documentation failures

Most early life O&M problems trace back to missing P&IDs, incomplete SOPs, or spares lists that never arrived with the plant.

Risk list essentials

Verify as built drawings, interlock logic, chemical SDS library, calibration certificates, and warranty contacts before the EPC team demobilizes.

First 30 days of O&M

Run the plant on the EPC SOPs first, then revise based on observed load. Do not rewrite everything on day one.

Practical checklist

  • Complete as built P&IDs and interlock register sign off.
  • Transfer spares, tools, and calibration records in one package.
  • Run joint walkthroughs with EPC and O&M shift leads.
  • Schedule a 30 day performance review with action log.
