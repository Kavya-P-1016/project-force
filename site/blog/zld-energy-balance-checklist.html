ZLD energy balance checklist before final technology freeze

A practical pre finalization checklist to validate thermal loads, utility tie ins, and operating risk in high recovery ZLD projects.

Why energy balance comes first

Before you lock a ZLD technology train, validate how much steam, power, and cooling the site can actually deliver at peak load. Most redesigns happen because the utility envelope was never reconciled with the process block diagram.

Checklist items that prevent rework

Confirm feed TDS variability, condensate recovery paths, heat integration between pretreatment and evaporators, and standby utility capacity. Map each block to a measured load, not a vendor brochure value.

Operating risk review

Run a failure mode review for single points of utility dependency. If one boiler trip shuts concentration, document mitigation before purchase orders go out.

Practical checklist

  • Validate steam and power peaks against site utility logs.
  • Reconcile heat duty across pretreatment, RO, and evaporator blocks.
  • Check condensate return quality and corrosion risk in reuse loops.
  • Define acceptance tests tied to energy intensity, not just recovery.
