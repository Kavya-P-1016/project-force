Predictive maintenance for treatment assets with limited budgets

How to prioritize motors, pumps, and rotating equipment for reliability gains without overbuilding your maintenance program.

Start with criticality, not technology

Before buying sensors everywhere, rank assets by consequence of failure. A blower on a single train ETP outranks a secondary transfer pump with a spare.

Low cost leading indicators

Vibration trends, motor current signatures, bearing temperature, and run hour logs already exist on many plants. Use them consistently before adding new systems.

Maintenance prioritization

Convert predictive alerts into weekly work orders with clear deferral rules. An alert without a workflow is just noise.

Practical checklist

  • Rank assets by compliance and production impact.
  • Track vibration and current trends on top ten critical assets.
  • Define alert thresholds with maintenance, not only automation vendors.
  • Review deferred alerts in weekly operations meetings.
